Common questions

When do the days open?

One window a day, December 1–31, by your device’s date. Before December 1 the app shows a countdown; after the season, every day stays open as a keepsake.

I missed a day. Can I catch up?

Yes. Open the Calendar and tap any day that has already arrived.

How do I turn the daily reminder on or off?

Settings → Daily reminder. If it won’t turn on, allow notifications for Winter Corner in your phone’s settings.

Will I lose my progress?

Progress is saved on your device only. Deleting the app deletes it; there is no account or cloud sync.
