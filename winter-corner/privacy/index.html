Winter Corner: Cozy Advent

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

Who we are

Winter Corner is made by AE Craft Studio, an independent developer. In this policy “we” and “us” mean AE Craft Studio. Contact: aecraft.studio@yandex.by.

The short version

Winter Corner doesn’t collect, send, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. Everything you do in the app stays on your device.

  • No accounts
  • No ads
  • No analytics
  • No tracking
  • Works offline

What the app keeps on your device

To remember your December, the app saves on your device: which days you opened and completed, the color you picked for each day, the fortunes you cracked, the tasks you swapped, and your settings (sound and music, vibration, falling snow, the daily reminder and its time). A finished season is kept on the device as a keepsake when a new one starts.

This is stored in the app’s private storage on your device. It is never uploaded to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it. If you use your device’s own backups (such as iCloud or Google backup), they may include this data under your device provider’s terms.

Notifications

The daily reminder is optional and off by default. If you turn it on, the app asks for permission to show notifications and schedules them on your device. No server is involved and nothing is sent anywhere.

Internet and third parties

The app works fully offline: its art, music, fonts, and content are built in, and it makes no network requests. It contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking code.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here and in the app, with a new date. Questions? Write to aecraft.studio@yandex.by or use the support contact on the app’s App Store or Google Play page.
